Is This Community For You?

This Is For You If

  • You're building something, even if it's just yourself, and want people around who get that
  • You're honest about where you're starting from, confused, a beginner, mid-figuring-it-out, all of it counts
  • You'll actually show up when something happens, not just lurk and collect links
  • You're willing to ask the obvious question instead of sitting stuck in silence

Skip This If

  • You want a guaranteed curriculum before you'll commit to anything
  • You're only here to take, never to add anything back to the room
  • You're not ready to be seen still figuring things out in front of others
  • You need this to fix your life for you instead of being the place you do the work

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This is a plain-language explanation of what happens to your details when you sign up for GovernorHQ. I'd rather you know exactly what I do with them than have to guess.

Who is responsible for your details

GovernorHQ is run by Govtech Enterprises. That's who decides how your details are used. To reach us about anything on this page, message Governor on WhatsApp: +234 813 828 1223.

What we collect

When you sign up on the page, we collect:

  • Your name or username
  • Your email address
  • Your WhatsApp number
  • Your country and state (optional)
  • What you hope to get out of GovernorHQ (optional)
  • The date and time you ticked the agreement box

If you join our WhatsApp group, WhatsApp itself shows your name and number to the group admin. That part is governed by WhatsApp's own terms.

Why we collect it

  • To approve you into the community and message you about it
  • To send you a welcome email and updates about GovernorHQ
  • To understand who is joining, so the community can be useful to them

We do not sell your details. We do not share them with anyone for advertising.

Who handles your details for us

To run the page, we use a few services that process your details on our behalf:

  • Supabase, where the sign-up list is stored
  • Brevo, which sends the emails
  • Vercel, which hosts this website
  • WhatsApp, where we chat and run the group

Some of these services keep data on servers outside Nigeria. We only use them for the purposes above.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while the community is running or until you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first.

Your choices

Under Nigeria's data protection law (the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023), you can:

  • Ask what details we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct them
  • Ask us to delete them
  • Take back your agreement and stop the emails and messages at any time

Just message us on WhatsApp using the number above and say what you'd like. If you are not happy with how we handled your details, you also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Younger members

Everyone is welcome, whatever your age. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before you sign up, because we rely on their permission for you to join.

Changes to this notice

If this notice changes, the date at the top will change with it.
